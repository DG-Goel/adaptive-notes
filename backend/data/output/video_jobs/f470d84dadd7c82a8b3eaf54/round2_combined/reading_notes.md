## DD curve

- The 45-degree line represents equilibrium where output equals demand; the DD curve shows demand for domestically produced goods.

## ZZ curve

- The ZZ curve is the adjusted demand curve after accounting for imports and exports, used to find domestic equilibrium output.
- Exports add a parallel shift to the demand curve and depend on foreign output, not changing its slope.

## equilibrium condition

- Equilibrium is when output (y) equals domestic demand plus exports minus imports (z).

## Net exports

- Net exports increase when autonomous foreign output (y*) rises and decrease when domestic output rises, because higher domestic output raises imports.
